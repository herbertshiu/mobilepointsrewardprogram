KEYWORD · Loyalty program software

Customer loyalty program software that staff will actually use

28 Sep 2026

Loyalty software only works when earning and redeeming fit a busy counter. Feature lists matter less than a clean visit loop.

Outcomes to optimize for

  • Higher 7–30 day repeat-visit rate
  • Reward redemptions that feel fair, not unreachable
  • Campaigns you can measure (issue → redeem → return)

Buying checklist

  1. Can a new hire learn earn/burn the same day?
  2. Does checkout stay under ~10 seconds in a rush?
  3. Can you export data if you switch later?
  4. Are SMS/push/email costs transparent?

Restaurants and cafés often need both customer-facing loyalty and light CRM segmentation—see also restaurant CRM and Mobile.Cards.

Measure the loop, not vanity installs

App downloads and total members look impressive but do not pay rent. Track earn events per day, redemption rate, and return visits after a reward. Loyalty software should make those three numbers easy to pull every Monday morning.
