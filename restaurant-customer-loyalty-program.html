KEYWORD · Restaurant loyalty program

Restaurant customer loyalty program: make the next visit obvious

28 Sep 2026

Guests should understand the benefit before the receipt prints. Keep rules short, rewards tasty, and staff prompts automatic.

Choose a simple mechanic

  • Stamps: easiest to explain for single-category menus
  • Points: better when average tickets vary widely
  • Member price or free add-on: strong when margins allow

Operate it like a shift checklist

  1. Invite at payment—every eligible ticket
  2. Earn in under ten seconds
  3. Surface progress (“2 visits to a free side”)
  4. Win back quiet dayparts with targeted offers

Tooling often blends loyalty + light restaurant CRM. For branded apps, evaluate options such as Mobile.Cards; for Chinese playbooks start at guides.
